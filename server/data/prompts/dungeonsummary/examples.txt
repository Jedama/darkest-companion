# Bursar Examples

## Example 1: Antiquarian as Bursar (Arrival Expedition)

**Context:** Josephine the Antiquarian is Bursar. She was NOT on the expedition. The Heiress, Heir, Highwayman, and Crusader arrived at the Estate via stagecoach, surviving a crash, two brigand ambushes, and a grueling march. The Crusader sustained a serious gunshot wound. The Heir was concussed and froze during combat. The Heiress commanded throughout. The Highwayman spotted threats, killed, and carried the wounded. Total loot: 6020 gold.

```json
{
  "headline": "The Heirs Arrive — Salvage Assessment",
  "characters": [
    {
      "identifier": "heiress",
      "points": ["Born to command", "Dangerous when crossed", "Controls the purse"],
      "share": 1280
    },
    {
      "identifier": "kheir",
      "points": ["Fragile but useful", "Manageable"],
      "share": 850
    },
    {
      "identifier": "highwayman",
      "points": ["Quiet", "Competent", "Professional"],
      "share": 1150
    },
    {
      "identifier": "crusader",
      "points": ["Unimaginative but functional", "Durable", "Watch the pious ones"],
      "share": 1100
    }
  ],
  "town": 1420,
  "bursar": 220,
  "justification": ""
}
```

**Explanation:** Josephine is calculating and politically cautious — she thinks several moves ahead and won't antagonize anyone who might damage her position. The Heiress gets the largest share not for bleeding the most, but because Josephine won't cross the person who controls her appointment. The Heir gets the smallest share but not conspicuously so — Josephine knows that halving someone's pay next to their sister creates an enemy, and she doesn't make enemies she doesn't have to. Dismas edges out Reynauld because Josephine pays for usefulness, not suffering. She skims 220 — modest, early, defensible as administrative compensation. Town gets a healthy share because she's protecting the infrastructure that makes future profit possible.

## Example 2: Cursed (Genn Greymane) as Bursar (Same Arrival Expedition)

**Context:** Same expedition as Scenario 1. Genn Greymane is Bursar. He was NOT on the expedition. Total loot: 6020 gold.

```json
    },
    {
      "identifier": "crusader",
      "points": ["Knows his duty", "Will not break"],
      "share": 1420
    }
  ],
  "town": 1500,
  "bursar": 0,
  "justification": ""
}
```

**Explanation:** Genn is a king in exile — blunt, duty-bound, and constitutionally unable to soften a verdict to spare feelings. The Crusader gets the largest share because Genn rewards sacrifice and duty above all. The Heir gets openly the smallest — Genn judges by merit, not bloodline, and doesn't dress the judgment up. Contrast with Josephine: she's the more careful political operator, he's blunter but arguably more honest. Town gets the single largest allocation because a king thinks in collective infrastructure first. No bursar cut — a king does not skim from his people's coffers.

## Example 3: Zenith (Syndoriel) as Bursar (Weald Expedition)

**Context:** Syndoriel the Zenith is Bursar. He WAS on the expedition. A four-person team (Zenith, Highwayman, Thorn, Bounty Hunter) completed a two-day expedition into the Weald. Combat was frequent and successful. Tardif the Bounty Hunter was brutally efficient throughout. Marlene the Thorn fought with precision using auditory cues. Dismas the Highwayman performed reliably but contracted Hashimoto's disease during the trip. Syndoriel kept morale high and fought with acrobatic flair. No serious injuries. Total loot: 13500 gold.

```json
{
  "headline": "Weald Run: Good Fight, Bad Luck",
  "characters": [
    {
      "identifier": "zenith",
      "points": ["Kept morale up", "Inspiring spearwork", "Natural leader", "Everyone's favourite", "Heart of the team"],
      "share": 3300
    },
    {
      "identifier": "highwayman",
      "points": ["Good company", "Get him a doctor"],
      "share": 2950
    },
    {
      "identifier": "thorn",
      "points": ["Graceful", "Doesn't miss", "Think she likes my voice"],
      "share": 2750
    },
    {
      "identifier": "bounty_hunter",
      "points": ["Effective", "Wish he'd talk more"],
      "share": 2200
    }
  ],
  "town": 2200,
  "bursar": 100,
  "justification": "Bought everyone drinks!"
}
```

**Explanation:** Syndoriel is hedonistic and solipsistic — charming, charismatic, and genuinely convinced he is the heart of any room he walks into. Five self-assessment points, all about how others perceive him rather than tactical contribution — he genuinely believes he was the star. (If Josephine self-assessed she'd probably only write "present.") Dismas gets the second-highest share — Syndoriel likes him AND feels sympathetic about the disease. Marlene gets a warm assessment; "Think she likes my voice" is in-character. Tardif gets the least despite arguably performing best — Syndoriel can't connect with silence, so his gut says Tardif deserves less. The bursar cut is 100 gold for a round of drinks. The justification field is optional: present when the Bursar would explain themselves, empty when they wouldn't.
